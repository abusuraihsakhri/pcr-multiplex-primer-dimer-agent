Multiplex PCR primer complementarity

Screen self- and cross-dimer candidates across a primer panel. Results are heuristic, not predicted thermodynamic stability or experimental performance.

Primer panel

One primer per line: name,sequence. Optional header. Unambiguous DNA (A/C/G/T), 4–80 nt, maximum 100 primers.

The uploaded file is read only on this device; it is not submitted to a server.

Interpretation: A contiguous complementary stretch is counted across antiparallel ungapped alignments. The 3′ score is the longest such stretch touching either primer’s 3′ terminus. Moderate: 3′ ≥4 nt or longest ≥7 nt. High: 3′ ≥5 nt or longest ≥9 nt. These are screening thresholds, not empirically calibrated probabilities.
